# Chipzo — Monetization Map (VLSI niche)

Four engines. They stack. Start at the top-left, add one per month.

| # | Engine | Start | Effort | Ceiling | Speed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Digital products | Month 2 | Low | ₹50k–2L/mo | Fast |
| 2 | Services (1:1) | Month 2 | High (time) | ₹40k–1.5L/mo | Fastest cash |
| 3 | Affiliate | Month 2 | Low | ₹10k–60k/mo | Slow |
| 4 | Sponsorship / ads | Month 4 | Low | ₹20k–1L/mo | Slow |

## 1. The offer ladder (the whole business in one picture)

```
FREE  ──► VLSI Interview Bank (120 Q&A PDF)        ← lead magnet, Telegram/email
FREE  ──► Daily Telegram/WhatsApp content           ← trust
₹149  ──► Interview Bank PRO (300 Q&A + answers)    ← impulse buy
₹299  ──► Cheatsheet Pack (STA / CDC / PD / DFT)    ← printable, high perceived value
₹499  ──► Resume + LinkedIn review                  ← service, 30 min
₹999  ──► Mock interview (45 min + written report)  ← service, best margin/hour
₹1,999──► "Get Hired in VLSI" bundle (all of it)    ← anchor price
₹4,999──► 4-session mentorship package              ← month 4+
```

**Why this order:** ₹149 is an impulse purchase for an engineering student. It
validates demand with zero risk. Everything above it exists to make ₹149 look cheap.

### Pricing psychology for this audience
- Students compare against **course fees (₹15,000–60,000)**. ₹149 feels free.
- Always show a **₹-per-question** or **₹-per-page** breakdown.
- Offer **"pay what you can"** on the ₹149 pack for the first 50 buyers — generates
  testimonials, which is the only thing that matters early.
- Never discount below ₹99. Free vs ₹99 is a different decision; ₹99 vs ₹49 is noise.

## 2. Digital products — what to build (in this order)

### Product 1: Interview Bank PRO — ₹149
- 300 questions across RTL, DV, STA, PD, DFT, CMOS, with short answers.
- Build it by **publishing 5 questions a day for 60 days**, then compiling.
  Free content builds the audience; the compilation is the product. Zero extra work.
- Format: PDF + a Notion/Google-Docs mirror. Deliver by email link after payment.

### Product 2: Cheatsheet Pack — ₹299
- 6 one-page A3/A4 sheets: STA formulas, CDC techniques, PD flow, DFT scan,
  low-power techniques, Verilog/SystemVerilog syntax.
- These get **printed and pinned on desks** → free word-of-mouth + they photograph
  well for Instagram (which markets them for you).

### Product 3: Project Starter Kits — ₹399
- Verilog testbenches, a UVM skeleton, a small RTL project with a spec.
- Freshers desperately need "something to show on my resume".
- Costs you one weekend of work. Evergreen seller.

**Delivery (all free):**
- Gumroad — 10% fee, no monthly cost, handles file delivery + VAT. Start here.
- Razorpay Payment Links — ~2% + GST, UPI-native, better for India. Add at ₹5k/mo.
- Google Drive + manual link send — 0% fee, doesn't scale past ~20 orders.

## 3. Services — the fastest cash

| Service | Price | Time | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Resume + LinkedIn review | ₹499 | 30 min | Written feedback doc, 3 revisions. Highest volume. |
| Mock interview (RTL/DV) | ₹999 | 45 min + 30 min report | Ask for a testimonial every time. |
| "Roadmap" call | ₹699 | 30 min | For career-switchers. Easy to sell. |
| Project review | ₹799 | 45 min | For final-year students. |

**Booking:** Cal.com free tier (unlimited 1:1) + Razorpay link in the confirmation.
**Capacity:** cap at 4/week so content doesn't die. Raise price, don't add hours.

**The pitch that converts** (put this in the pinned post + blog page):
> "I review 20+ VLSI resumes a month. The same 5 mistakes kill 90% of fresher
> applications. ₹499 gets you a line-by-line fix + a rewritten summary + 3 rounds
> of revisions. If you don't get more callbacks in 30 days, full refund."

Refund promise costs you almost nothing and doubles conversion.

## 4. Affiliate — set up once, earn passively

| Program | Commission | Fit |
|---|---|---|
| Udemy / Coursera / edX affiliates | 15–45% | "Best VLSI course for X" posts |
| Amazon Associates (India) | 0.2–9% | Books: CMOS VLSI Design, Verilog HDL, UVM Primer |
| VLSI training institutes (direct) | ₹1,000–5,000/lead | Negotiate directly via DM |
| Hosting/tools (DigitalOcean, Notion) | $25–$50/signup | Only for "how to build X" posts |
| Cadence/Synopsys — no. | — | Enterprise only, skip |

**Rule:** only recommend what you'd recommend free. One bad affiliate post
destroys a small niche reputation permanently.

## 5. Sponsorship — the real money at 2k+ followers

VLSI edtech spends aggressively on Telegram/LinkedIn because the audience is
narrow and high-value. At 1,500 Telegram subs you can charge ₹1,500–3,000 per post.

**Rate card (start here, raise quarterly):**
```
Telegram channel post ............ ₹1,500
Telegram post + pinned 48h ....... ₹2,500
LinkedIn post .................... ₹2,000
Instagram carousel + story ....... ₹1,500
Blog sponsored article ........... ₹3,000
Newsletter (per 1k sends) ........ ₹1,000
Bundle (all channels, 1 week) .... ₹8,000
```
**Who to pitch:** VLSI training institutes, EDA tool course sellers, semiconductor
recruitment consultancies, hardware bootcamps, textbook publishers, chip-design
YouTube channels.

**Pitch template:**
> Hi [Name] — Chipzo reaches [X] VLSI students/freshers across Telegram ([X]),
> LinkedIn ([X]) and Instagram ([X]), 78% India, mostly final-year + 0–2 yr
> engineers actively job-hunting. I have a slot next week. Happy to send a
> 1-page media kit with engagement numbers. Interested?

## 6. Revenue model (month 6, realistic)

```
Digital products      15 sales/mo × ₹200 avg  = ₹3,000
Services               6/mo × ₹800 avg        = ₹4,800
Affiliate                                     = ₹2,000
Sponsorship            2/mo × ₹2,500          = ₹5,000
                                    TOTAL     ≈ ₹14,800/mo
```

Month 12 with 15k followers and a ₹999 course: ₹40k–1L/mo is normal in this niche.
The bottleneck is never the market — it's publishing consistently for 6 months.

## 7. Non-negotiables

- **Payment proof → testimonial → public.** Every single buyer, ask.
- **One product page, one CTA.** No 6-way choice paralysis.
- **Price in ₹ and show UPI.** Students don't have international cards.
- **Never sell a course before you have a service buyer.** Service first = you
  learn what people actually struggle with, then package it.
- **Keep the free tier genuinely good.** The paid thing should be *more*, not
  *the good part held hostage*.
