## Voice rules

**Do**
- Lead with a specific number, claim, or question.
- Name the concept precisely (setup time, not "timing stuff").
- Use "you", never "one".
- Max 1 emoji per 3 lines. Usually zero.
- Give the answer, then the reason.

**Don't**
- "In today's fast-paced world..."
- "Let's dive in", "game-changer", "unlock", "delve"
- Motivational filler ("consistency is key 🔥")
- Fake authority ("As an industry expert...")
- Emoji bullets on every line

**Swap table**

| Instead of | Write |
|---|---|
| "It is very important to understand..." | "This decides whether your chip works." |
| "Many freshers struggle with..." | "80% of freshers get this wrong in interviews." |
| "Let's understand setup time" | "Setup time is a promise you make to the clock." |

## Visual identity (all free, all in Canva)

- **Primary:** `#0B1020` (near-black navy)
- **Accent:** `#00E5A0` (signal green) — use sparingly, one element per slide
- **Secondary:** `#F5F7FA` (off-white text on dark)
- **Warning/attention:** `#FFB020`

**Fonts (Google Fonts, free):** `Inter` for body, `JetBrains Mono` for code and
timing diagrams. These two are all you need.

**Carousel template (1080×1350)**
```
[top-left]  CHIPZO
[slide N]   small, top-right
[headline]  48–64pt Inter Bold, max 8 words
[body]      24–28pt Inter Regular, max 3 lines
[bottom]    chipzo.blogspot.com  ·  @chipzo
```

**Profile picture:** `C` monogram in Inter Bold, `#0B1020` background,
`#00E5A0` letter. Make it once, use it everywhere, never change it.

**Cover/banner:** dark navy, accent line, the one-liner, and the lead magnet URL.
