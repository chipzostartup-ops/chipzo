## Rules for using these

1. Never use two hooks in one post. Pick one, deliver on it.
2. The hook must be **true for your post**. Clickbait in a small niche is fatal.
3. Rewrite hook #2 of every list to be more specific than #1.
4. Keep a `winners.md` — every hook that performed 2x your average goes there,
   and you reuse the *structure* with new topics.
