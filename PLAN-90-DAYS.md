# Chipzo — 90 Day Plan (₹0 budget)

**Niche:** VLSI / semiconductor. India-first audience (huge fresher + job-switcher
volume, English-speaking, extremely active on LinkedIn and Telegram).

**Who you serve:** B.Tech/M.Tech ECE/EEE students, VLSI trainees, 0–3 yr
engineers in RTL design, DV, PD, DFT, STA, layout.

**The unfair advantage:** this audience has *money-adjacent pain* — jobs,
interviews, courses, career switches. Content that helps them get hired converts
far better than generic tech content.

## Phase 0 — Foundation (Days 1–7)

Do **not** post yet. Build the machine first, or you'll burn out in week 3.

- [ ] Pick the brand line. Suggested:
      *"Chipzo — VLSI, explained without the gatekeeping."*
- [ ] Register handles **@chipzo** everywhere (same name, same photo, same bio —
      see `brand/brand-kit.md`). Consistency is what makes a tiny brand look real.
- [ ] Blogger blog live at `chipzo.blogspot.com` → later map a free-ish domain.
      (Custom domain ≈ ₹800/yr — the *only* thing worth spending on, and only
      after month 2. Until then `.blogspot.com` is fine for SEO.)
- [ ] Create: Telegram channel, WhatsApp Channel, Facebook Page, Instagram
      Business account, LinkedIn Page + your personal profile as the main engine.
- [ ] Make the **lead magnet**: "VLSI Interview Bank — 120 questions with
      answers" (PDF). Build it from your own notes; 25–40 pages.
- [ ] Set up Brevo (free 300 emails/day) for the email list.
- [ ] Drop a Telegram/WhatsApp CTA in every bio: `Free VLSI Interview Bank → link`.

**Deliverable by Day 7:** 5 platforms live, 1 lead magnet, 1 pinned "start here"
post on each.

## Phase 1 — Proof & Volume (Days 8–30)

**Goal:** find the 3 content formats that get shared, and publish enough to know.

Cadence (realistic for 1 person, ~2 hrs/day):

| Platform | Cadence | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Blog | 3 / week | 45 min each (engine does the rest) |
| LinkedIn (personal) | 1 / day, weekdays | 15 min |
| Instagram | 3 / week (1 reel, 2 carousels) | 30 min |
| Telegram | 1 / day (repurposed) | 2 min |
| WhatsApp Channel | 3 / week | 2 min |
| Facebook | 2 / week + 5 group posts/week | 20 min |
| Email | 1 / week | 10 min |

**Content pillars (rotate):**
1. **Interview prep** — "Top 20 STA questions", "CDC questions asked at Qualcomm"
2. **Concept explainers** — setup/hold, latch vs flop, CDC, low-power, DFT basics
3. **Career** — "RTL vs DV: which pays more in 2026", "how to switch from testing to VLSI"
4. **Industry/company** — hiring news, salary bands, layoffs, fab updates
5. **Behind the scenes** — your own learning, projects, mistakes

Ratio: 50% pillars 1–2, 30% pillar 3, 20% pillars 4–5.

**Weekly loop (boring on purpose):**
- Mon: pick 3 topics from `content/ideas.csv`
- Mon–Wed: write 3 source files → `repurpose.py` → schedule the week
- Daily: 20 min commenting on VLSI hiring posts on LinkedIn (this is 50% of growth)
- Fri: check dashboard, kill formats that flopped, double the ones that worked

**Kill criteria:** after 20 posts, if a format gets <2% engagement twice, drop it.

## Phase 2 — Monetize small (Days 31–60)

Ship the cheapest possible paid thing. The goal is *proof someone pays*, not revenue.

- [ ] **Product 1 — ₹99–₹199:** "VLSI Interview Bank Pro" (200 Q&A + answers,
      PDF). Sell on Gumroad (free, 10% fee) or Razorpay Payment Links.
- [ ] **Product 2 — ₹299–₹499:** "STA / CDC / PD Cheatsheet Pack" (printable).
- [ ] **Service 1 — ₹499:** Resume + LinkedIn review for VLSI freshers.
- [ ] **Service 2 — ₹999–₹1,499:** 45-min mock interview with written feedback.
- [ ] Add a "Work with me" page on the blog + link in every bio.
- [ ] Start a **Telegram paid/premium tier** later — for now just build the free one.
- [ ] Apply to 2 affiliate programs: Udemy/edX course affiliates, VLSI training
      institutes, Amazon books ("CMOS VLSI Design" etc.).

**Conversion mechanic that works:** every free lead magnet ends with
*"Want the answers + 80 more? → Pro pack, ₹149."*

**Expected:** 2–10 sales of ₹149 + 2–4 resume reviews = ₹3k–8k.

## Phase 3 — Scale & systemize (Days 61–90)

- [ ] **SEO compounding:** find the 10 posts with impressions but low CTR
      (Google Search Console, free) and rewrite titles. This is the highest-ROI hour
      of the whole 90 days.
- [ ] **Internal linking:** every post links to 3 others + 1 product page.
- [ ] **Repurpose winners:** take the top 3 posts and make them into a free
      mini-course delivered by email (7 days) → sells the paid pack on day 8.
- [ ] **Collabs:** 2 guest posts on bigger VLSI blogs, 2 Telegram cross-promos
      with similar-size channels, 1 LinkedIn live/audio event with a working engineer.
- [ ] **Sponsorship-ready deck:** 1-page PDF with audience numbers + rate card.
      VLSI edtech pays ₹2k–10k per Telegram/LinkedIn post at 2k–5k followers.
- [ ] **Batching:** one 4-hour Sunday session writes the whole week. Protect it.

## The distribution loops (memorize these)

**Loop A — LinkedIn → Telegram → Blog**
Post a strong carousel on LinkedIn → CTA "full explanation + PDF in Telegram" →
Telegram post links to the blog → blog captures email → email sells.

**Loop B — Facebook Groups → WhatsApp Channel**
Answer VLSI questions in groups with genuinely complete answers → soft link to
channel → channel gets the daily repurposed content.

**Loop C — Google → Blog → Email**
Long-tail "X interview questions" pages rank in 3–6 months → lead magnet → email.

**Loop D — Instagram Reels → Profile → Link**
Reels of "draw this timing diagram in 30s" style. Highest reach per minute of
effort; lowest conversion. Use it for reach only.

## Failure modes to avoid

1. **Posting on 6 platforms daily with the same generic text.** Repurpose ≠
   repost. The engine already rewrites per platform. Use it.
2. **Waiting for the logo, website, or domain.** Blogspot + default fonts is fine.
3. **Teaching theory nobody searches for.** Every post should answer a question a
   student would type into Google at 2am before an interview.
4. **Ignoring comments.** 30 thoughtful comments/day beat 3 extra posts.
5. **Monetizing too late.** You should ask for ₹99 in week 6, not month 9.
6. **No owned list.** If Telegram dies you still have email. Build both.
